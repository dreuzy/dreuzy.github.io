Research · further reading

Scientific results and contributions

A selection of questions that have structured my research. Each entry leads to a more detailed presentation of the question, the approach, the main results and the associated contributions.

01 · Shallow aquifers and headwater catchments
What can streams tell us about the subsurface?
Using stream-network extent and intermittency to constrain the hydraulic properties of poorly observed aquifers.
02 · Residence times, water quality and reactivity
Why is residence time not enough to predict contaminant fate?
Linking flow structures, time spent in the subsurface and the location of reactive zones, especially to understand denitrification.
03 · Water resources in a changing environment
How does climate change concretely transform water resources?
Connecting climate projections to low flows, intermittency, storage, water supply and coastal groundwater.
04 · Modelling and upscaling
From catchment to Europe: which model for which question?
Making models comparable, reproducible and deployable, then adapting their level of fidelity to geology, data and simulation objectives.
05 · Fractured media, heterogeneity and upscaling
Which fracture-network properties actually control flow and transport?
Distinguishing controls on mean permeability from those on channeling, transport and reactivity in strongly heterogeneous media.
06 · Current perspective
How does a hydrosystem transform a pressure into a real impact?
A question now developed within OneWater PC4, linking pressures, transfers, transformations, exposures, effects and uses.
