Groundwater modelling · Python

HydroModPy

HydroModPy is a Python toolbox for building, running, calibrating and analysing catchment-scale shallow-groundwater models in a reproducible way.

The workflow automates catchment delineation; the preparation of spatial and temporal data; model parameterisation; MODFLOW simulations; particle tracking and solute transport; and the export and visualisation of results.

Groundwater-age interpretation · Python

PyAge

PyAge is an extensible Python environment for interpreting groundwater ages using environmental tracers and lumped-parameter models.

Its architecture separates tracers, transit-time distributions, convolution operators and calibration methods. Bayesian calibration with Metropolis–Hastings is used to explore uncertainty, parameter trade-offs and identifiability limits rather than reducing the analysis to a single deterministic solution.
