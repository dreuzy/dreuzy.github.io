Projects

Our research is structured around collaborative projects that connect process understanding, model development and water-management challenges.

ANR PRCI · 2026–2030

FutureFlow

Scientific coordination: Jean-Raynald de Dreuzy & Clément Roques · University of Neuchâtel

FutureFlow develops a multi-model, multi-fidelity approach to better represent groundwater contributions to headwater-catchment dynamics.

We aim to select the appropriate level of model fidelity for the dominant processes, available data and simulation objectives, then deploy these approaches across a large number of European catchments.

Participants — Arnaud Blouin, Benoît Combemale, Alexandre Boisson, Florence Habets, Oliver Schilling…

Institutions — CNRS / University of Rennes · University of Neuchâtel · BRGM · ENS-PSL · University of Basel / Eawag

Funding — ANR PRCI France–Switzerland

PEPR OneWater · PC4 · 2022–2031

Water Footprint & Sentinel Water

Scientific coordination: Jean-Raynald de Dreuzy & Hélène Budzinski · University of Bordeaux

We investigate how socio-hydrological systems transform, attenuate, delay or reveal pressures on water, linking water uses, transfers, exposure, observed effects and indicators.

Phase 2 brings together four complementary projects — AMONT, HOLI-AQUA, SEREINE and SPeCtre — and a broad scientific and stakeholder community.

Funding — France 2030 · PEPR OneWater – Eau Bien Commun · ANR

University of Rennes Foundation · 2019–2028

Chaire Eaux & Territoires

Co-chairs: Luc Aquilina & Jean-Raynald de Dreuzy · University of Rennes / CNRS

Working with water managers and local authorities, we investigate how water resources respond to climate change and changing demands, connecting surface water, groundwater, water quality and water-supply systems.

Funding — Eau du Bassin Rennais · Rennes Métropole (2019–2028) · SMG Eau 35 (2024–2028)

Other current projects

SHALCC — 2025–2028
Shallow groundwater resources under Climate Change
Lead — Nicolas Massei · M2C
Funding — ANR ICCER
Hybrid physics-based/deep-learning modelling of shallow aquifers under climate change, from gauged to ungauged catchments, with M2C, Géosciences Rennes, RiverLy, SCE and LJAD.
NIRECAS — 2024–2026
NItrate REcovery CApacity Status
Lead — Flore Sergeant · Marie Skłodowska-Curie Postdoctoral Fellowship
Funding — European Union · MSCA Postdoctoral Fellowship
Assessing and mapping nitrate recovery capacity by linking reactivity, agricultural legacy, transit times and lithology across more than 300 pilot catchments in France and Germany, before extending the approach to other European regions.

Earlier projects — selection

RIVAGES Normands 2100 — 2017–2025
Lead — Jean-Raynald de Dreuzy & Luc Aquilina, Géosciences Rennes
Funding — DREAL Normandy · Seine-Normandie Water Agency · local authorities
Study of chronic groundwater rise, flooding and coastal-aquifer salinisation under sea-level rise and climate change. The programme combined field observations, hydrogeological modelling and dialogue with coastal territories in Normandy.
EAUX 2050 / EAUX2070 & CYDRE — 2019–2024
Lead — Jean-Raynald de Dreuzy, with BRGM and water-management partners in Brittany
Funding — Brittany Region · BRGM · subsequent CNRS / Brittany Region support
Assessment of groundwater-resource evolution to 2050–2100 under climate and human pressures, with drinking-water syndicates involved in the programme. CYDRE extended this work into an operational tool for seasonal forecasting of streamflow and water-resource availability.
MORAQUI — 2018–2020
Lead — Luc Aquilina · modelling responsibility: Jean-Raynald de Dreuzy
Funding — French State / Brittany Region · “Algues vertes” programme
Study of aquifer reactivity, nitrate transfers and catchment response times to changes in agricultural practices. The project linked groundwater circulation, denitrification and the evolution of water quality in catchments in Brittany.
AquiFR-BZH — 2014–2019
Lead — Jean-Raynald de Dreuzy · Géosciences Rennes
Funding — AFB / Brittany Region
Regional modelling of basement aquifers and surface–groundwater exchanges from hydrological and geological data available at large scales. The project funded Quentin Courtois's PhD and contributed to the national AquiFR platform led by Florence Habets.
H2MNO4 — 2012–2016
Lead — Inria · work-package lead and Rennes coordinator: Jean-Raynald de Dreuzy
Funding — ANR · numerical-model programme
Development of models and numerical methods for heterogeneous hydrogeology and reactive transport, with Inria, UMR P’, ANDRA and ITASCA. The project strengthened coupling, scientific-computing and software-architecture approaches used in fractured-media research.
MUIGECCOS — 2011–2013
Lead — Jean-Raynald de Dreuzy
Funding — European Union · Marie Curie Individual European Fellowship
Modelling of coupled processes with applications to geological CO₂ storage. The fellowship established the collaboration with CSIC and the Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya in Barcelona on reactive transport and modelling methods.
MOHINI — 2007–2010
Lead — BRGM · work-package lead and Rennes coordinator: Jean-Raynald de Dreuzy
Funding — ANR · Vulnerability, Environments and Climate programme
Integrated modelling of water resources in basement aquifers and their vulnerability to anthropogenic global change. The project marked an important step toward catchment-scale modelling of shallow aquifers, with field contexts including Brittany and India.
MICAS — 2007–2010
Lead — Inria · work-package lead and Rennes coordinator: Jean-Raynald de Dreuzy
Funding — ANR · High-performance computing and simulation
Development of high-performance computing methods to simulate flow and transport in heterogeneous and fractured aquifers. The project brought together geosciences, applied mathematics and parallel computing to solve models with very large numbers of degrees of freedom.
