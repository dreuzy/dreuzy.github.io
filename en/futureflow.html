ANR PRCI · ANR-25-CE01-2963 · 2026–

FutureFlow

A multi-fidelity approach for regionally assessing groundwater contributions to headwater catchments.

Why this project?

Headwater catchments drain about 60% of continental land surfaces, yet groundwater contributions to their functioning remain difficult to quantify at large scales. Detailed models represent processes more fully but require substantial data and computing resources; parsimonious models are easier to deploy but simplify the system.

FutureFlow will select and combine these levels of fidelity according to the scientific question, dominant processes and available observations, including in ungauged catchments.

Scientific programme

Compare

Evaluate several model families on documented catchments and identify their domains of validity.

Select

Automate selection of the appropriate fidelity level and quantify the associated uncertainty.

Deploy

Transfer the approaches to ensembles of European catchments, including ungauged ones.
