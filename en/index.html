École normale supérieure de Rennes

I work on water resources and groundwater flow, from hillslope to regional scales. My research combines hydrogeology, hydrology and modeling to understand water and contaminant transfers, their relationships with geological and geomorphological structures, and their evolution under climate change.

Current and former responsibilities: President of ENS Rennes (since 2026); Vice-President for Research at ENS Rennes (2022–2026); Director of the Rennes Observatory of Sciences of the Universe (2017–2021); Head of the interdisciplinary RISC-E group and of the Hydrogeology group at Géosciences Rennes (2013–2016).
