Normandy · transdisciplinary, place-based research

Coastal risks: from RIVAGES to ARCHANGE

Two successive programmes examining how sea-level rise and changing rainfall alter coastal aquifers, groundwater-flooding risk and saltwater intrusion.

2019–2025

RIVAGES Normands 2100

RIVAGES combined groundwater monitoring, mapping, modelling and dialogue with local authorities to characterize groundwater rise and coastal-aquifer salinization. Phase I ran from 2019 to 2023 and phase II from 2023 to 2025.

Scientific leads: Jean-Raynald de Dreuzy and Luc Aquilina · Partners: OSERen, University of Caen Normandy, EHESP, Seine-Normandie Water Agency, DREAL Normandy and local authorities.

Open maps and data · 2026 synthesis paper

2025–

ARCHANGE

ARCHANGE extends this work to the Bay of Mont-Saint-Michel. It examines the combined effects of sea-level rise and changing precipitation on shallow groundwater beneath polders, marshes and estuaries through 2100.

Scientific contacts: Luc Aquilina and Martin Le Mesnil. His work covers hydrogeological modelling, the piezometric network and saltwater intrusion.
