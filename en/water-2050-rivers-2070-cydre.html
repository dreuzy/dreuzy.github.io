Brittany · climate, water resources and decision-support tools

EAUX 2050, RIVIÈRES 2070 and CYDRE

A family of projects that advanced from long-term projections to an operational seasonal-forecasting service.

2019–2022

EAUX 2050

Assessing the evolution of groundwater resources in Brittany through 2050–2100 under climate and human pressures, together with drinking-water syndicates and CRESEB.

2020–2023

RIVIÈRES 2070

Exploring river trajectories and territorial water management under climate and human pressures through a participatory approach.

2023–2026 · contract LSP 259381

CYDRE

Turning this knowledge into seasonal forecasts of streamflow and water-resource availability. The project deployed and evaluated the workflow across 98 catchments and prepared its integration into a reproducible environment.

Partners: CNRS, BRGM, Brittany Region, European Union / ERDF and CRESEB.
